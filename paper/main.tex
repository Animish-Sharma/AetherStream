\begin{abstract}
We study bounded-memory predictive compression of floating-point telemetry under explicit wire-rate and pointwise-error constraints. The artifact combines closed-loop prediction, entropy-constrained scalar quantization, interleaved asymmetric numeral systems, indexed random access, physical-signal fidelity metrics, and hardware-counter profiling. All reported plots are generated from executable experiments and preserve the distinction between measured values, analytical bounds, and modeled operation counts.
\end{abstract}
