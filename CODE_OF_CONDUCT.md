# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AetherStream a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive technical feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance of correction, attention to community impact, and credit for others' work. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or imagery, insults, trolling, threats, publication of private information, sustained disruption, or conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may issue a private warning, temporary suspension, or permanent ban according to severity and pattern of behavior. Enforcement decisions should protect reporters' privacy and avoid conflicts of interest.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@aetherstream.dev`. Reports are acknowledged within 48 hours. Maintainers will investigate promptly, document decisions privately, and disclose only what is necessary to protect the community. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html. Enforcement guidance follows https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html#enforcement-guidelines.
